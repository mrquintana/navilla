## Design Principles

**Private by appearance.** The interface should feel contained. Nothing about the visual language should feel open, social, or casual.

**Serious without alarm.** The product deals with health exposure — potentially significant information. The design should convey gravity without creating anxiety.

**Built for daily use.** This is not a product people open once. The visual system should be comfortable enough to check regularly, especially in dark mode on mobile.

**Ownable at scale.** Every color and typographic choice was evaluated not just for how it looks today, but for whether it can become recognizably Navilla in five years.
